Privacy Policy

G1 Ready Ontario

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how G1 Ready Ontario handles information when you use the Android app. The app provides independent study material for Ontario's G1 knowledge test.

Age choice

On first use, the app asks whether you are under 16 or 16 or older so it can decide whether to show ads. Your choice is stored only on your device. The app does not ask for your date of birth or name. You can change the choice in the app's Privacy settings. Learners under 16 can use the study features, but the advertising SDK is not initialized and no ads are requested for them.

Information stored on your device

Your practice answers, results, saved questions, sign reviews, appearance preference, and unfinished practice sessions are stored in the app on your device. We do not operate an account system or receive this learning data on our servers. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes its local data. A backup you export may remain with you or with an app you choose to share it with.

If you choose to export or import a backup, Android's file picker and sharing features let you select the file or destination. Those services and destinations are governed by their own privacy practices.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

For learners who select that they are 16 or older, the Android app may display a banner advertisement on the home screen. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and requests non-personalized ads. The SDK sends information directly to Google to deliver, measure, and protect ads. According to Google's current Google Play disclosure for the SDK, this may include your IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), interactions with the app, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. We do not use your study answers or progress to select ads.

Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Android also provides controls for resetting or deleting the advertising ID.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and display privacy choices where required. You can access available ad privacy choices from the app's About & sources screen.

External websites

Links to Ontario's official handbook and other references open outside the app, typically in your browser. The destination website may receive information such as your IP address and applies its own privacy policy.

Children

G1 Ready Ontario is designed for learners aged 13 and older who are preparing for Ontario's G1 knowledge test, including people studying before they become eligible to apply. Ontario's minimum age to apply for a G1 is 16. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children on its own servers. The age choice is stored on the device as described above; advertising is disabled for learners who choose under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
